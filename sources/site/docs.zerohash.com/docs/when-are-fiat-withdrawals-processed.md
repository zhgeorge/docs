# Source: https://docs.zerohash.com/docs/when-are-fiat-withdrawals-processed

As a standard, fiat and digital asset withdrawals are processed between the hours of 4:00pm and 5:00pm Central. Intraday withdrawals can also be requested and processed dependent on the status of a participant's Net Delivery Obligations (NDO) and current trading session activities.

For fiat wire withdrawals, zerohash is restricted to the following banking and federal wire cutoff times:

## 

USD Wire Withdrawals

- U.S. Domestic wires are processed until 4:30pm Central (for same day value date)
- International wires are processed until 4:30pm Central (for same day value date)

## 

Non-USD Fiat Wire Withdrawals

- Canadian dollar & Mexico peso withdrawals are processed until 3:00pm Central (for same day value date)
- Euro and British pound sterling withdrawals are processed from 7:30am Central - 8:30am Central (for same day value date)
- Other fiat currency withdrawals are processed from 7:30am Central - 4:30pm Central (for next day value)
