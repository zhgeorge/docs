# 

**Massachusetts**

**\[Kiosks Supported - Except for Woburn, Salem, and Burlington, MA\]**

## 

**Cryptocurrency Kiosk Ban**

### 

**Woburn, MA**

The City of Woburn, Massachusetts has banned all cryptocurrency kiosks from operating within the city. Zerohash will not support kiosk activity in Woburn, Massachusetts.

### 

**Salem, MA**

The City of Salem, Massachusetts has banned all cryptocurrency kiosks from operating within the city. Zerohash will not support kiosk activity in Salem, Massachusetts.

### 

**Burlington, MA**

The City of Burlington, Massachusetts has banned all cryptocurrency kiosks from operating within the city. Zerohash will not support kiosk activity in Burlington, Massachusetts.
