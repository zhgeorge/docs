zerohash's Central Limit Order Book (CLOB) enables Platforms to access digital asset trading with access to deep liquidity provided by the top market makers in the industry. Zerohash provides a fair and orderly market, ensuring efficient execution of orders based on price-time priority.

With zerohash’s CLOB, we offer a hassle-free trading experience that eliminates the need to manage relationships with multiple LPs. Provide your Customers with a reliable trading experience that includes a variety of order types and time in force (TIF) combinations.

To access zerohash's CLOB, Platforms can use our FIX API, which provides a standardized messaging protocol for real-time electronic exchange of trade-related messages. Our team at zerohash will configure and support clients during integration to ensure a smooth and secure connection. With the FIX API, Platforms can easily integrate their existing systems with zerohash's CLOB, enabling efficient and effective execution of trades across an ever-growing range of digital asset pairs.

## 

Accessing the CLOB

To integrate with zerohash's CLOB via our FIX API (see specification [here](https://docs.zerohash.com/reference/central-limit-order-book)), platforms must establish a FIX session with our system. The CLOB operates using the FIX 5.0 protocol — a widely adopted messaging standard in the financial industry — ensuring secure, reliable, and efficient communication between clients and our infrastructure.

zerohash will provide guidance and support throughout the onboarding process to ensure a smooth and successful integration. Our team will work closely with Platforms to configure their FIX session and ensure that it meets their specific requirements. We prioritize security and take measures to ensure that our Platform's data and assets are protected.

Please contact our client services team for additional details: [support@zerohash.com](mailto:support@zerohash.com)

## 

CLOB Members

These types require a platform that offers advanced order placement methods and deep liquidity, which Zerohash provides.

By leveraging our CLOB, our Platforms can offer their customers a seamless and hassle-free crypto trading experience. Our APIs allow for easy integration into existing systems, providing a cost-effective and efficient way to quickly launch a full crypto solution. Additionally, our financial reporting ensures that our Platforms have access to industry-standard information on all of our services, allowing them to make informed decisions and manage their portfolios effectively.

In summary, zerohash's CLOB is the ideal solution for institutions looking to offer advanced crypto trading services to their Platforms and source liquidity, while not being exposed to holding digital assets on their balance sheets. With deep liquidity, easy integration, and comprehensive financial reporting, our platform enables our Platforms to meet the growing demand for digital asset trading while minimizing risk and maximizing returns.
