## 

Create a Platform

All API, webhook, websocket, and SDK interactions stem from what we call a "Platform". A Platform represents the master account for your business and is your direct link to creating API keys and interacting with our stack.

We support both a **Cert** (or "Test") and a **Prod** environment. The Cert environment allows developers to simulate transactions using fake funds, accounts, participants, etc. By contrast, the Prod environment is meant for real-life transactions and is subject to various rules and regulations under which zerohash operates.

### 

Test Platform setup instructions

> 🧪
> 
> ### 
> 
> **Setting up your Cert account**
> 
> 1. Upon request, and after you've established contact with our sales or solutions engineering team, zerohash will create a Platform for you. They will add individuals from your business to the Platform. These are called [users](https://docs.zerohash.com/docs/how-to-add-user) .
> 
> 2. Attached users will be sent an email for verification. Click "Confirm my account" within the email body.
> 
> 3. You'll be prompted to create a username and password + register your 2FA device.
> 
> 4. You are now logged into the Cert **Client Portal**.
> 
> 5. You can now generate [API Keys](https://docs.zerohash.com/docs/generate-api-keys) and [Whitelist IP Addresses](https://docs.zerohash.com/docs/whitelist-ip-addresses)
> 

The Cert URL for the Client Portal is [portal.cert.zerohash.com](https://portal.cert.zerohash.com) in the US and [portal.cert.zerohash.eu](https://portal.cert.zerohash.eu/) in the EU.

The Cert URL for the API is [api.cert.zerohash.com](https://api.cert.zerohash.com) in the US and [api.cert.zerohash.eu](https://api.cert.zerohash.eu) in the EU.

### 

Prod Platform setup instructions

> 🏗️
> 
> ### 
> 
> **Setting up your Prod account**
> 
> 1. In contrast to Cert, you will need to initiate the Platform creation process yourself. Go to the [zerohash portal](https://portal.zerohash.com).
> 2. Click "**Sign Up**" - it will prompt you to create a username and password.
> 3. You'll be sent an email to verify your user account. Click "**Confirm my account**" on the email body.
> 4. You'll be prompted to register your 2FA device.
> 5. You are now logged into the Prod **Client Portal**.
> 6. Next, fill out your Platform's business information in the Application. Upon a successful submission, we'll be notified and begin reviewing the information.
> 7. When the review is complete, we'll approve the Platform account. You will receive an automated email confirming the approval.
> 8. Before generating API Keys and whitelisting IP addresses, we require your flow of funds to be reviewed and approved by our Compliance and Sales Engineering teams.
> 9. Upon approval, you can start generating [API Keys](https://docs.zerohash.com/docs/generate-api-keys) and [Whitelist IP Addresses](https://docs.zerohash.com/docs/whitelist-ip-addresses)

The Prod URL for the Client Portal is [portal.zerohash.com](https://portal.zerohash.com/) in the US and [portal.zerohash.eu](https://portal.zerohash.eu/) in the EU.

The Prod URL for the API is [api.zerohash.com](https://api.zerohash.com) in the US and [api.zerohash.eu](https://api.zerohash.eu) in the EU.
