## 

**Cryptocurrency Kiosk Ban**

### 

**Layton, UT**

The City of Layton, Utah has banned all cryptocurrency kiosks from operating within the city.  Zerohash will not support kiosk activity in Layton, Utah.

### 

**Herber City, UT**

The City of Herber City, Utah has banned all cryptocurrency kiosks from operating within the city.  Zerohash will not support kiosk activity in Herber City, Utah.
