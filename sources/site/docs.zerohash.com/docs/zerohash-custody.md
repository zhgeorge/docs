## 

Custody Services

_Our custody service provides a secure, regulated, and reliable solution for the safekeeping of digital assets, including a wide range of tokenized assets._

- Secure Safekeeping: Security is ensured through two key layers. Technologically, assets are protected by Multi-Party Computation (MPC) with a 3-of-3 threshold signature scheme. Operationally, our policies enforce a strict segregation of duties, ensuring there is no single point of failure.
- Policy Engine Integration: Granular control over token flows through address whitelisting, blacklisting, and configurable transfer limits.
- Segregated Accounts: Client assets are held in segregated accounts and are never commingled with company funds. Segregation can be configured at the unique wallet level or at the ledger level within an omnibus wallet structure.
- Robust Security & Audits: Our platform is SOC 1 Type II, SOC 2 Type II, and ISO 27001:2022 certified, and is uniquely Regulation SCI compliant.
- Regulated & Licensed: zerohash llc is a FinCEN-registered Money Service Business, a regulated Money Transmitter in 53 U.S. jurisdictions, and holds a New York BitLicense. zerohash trust is a registered Transfer Agent and a non-depository trust company chartered by the North Carolina Commissioner of Banks (NCCOB).

## 

Qualified Custody

> What is a Qualified Custodian?
> 
> A "Qualified Custodian" is a financial institution, like a trust company or bank, that is legally authorized to hold and safeguard a client's assets. This is a requirement for Registered Investment Advisers (RIAs) under the Investment Advisers Act of 1940 to protect client assets from loss or misuse.

### 

Qualified Custody with zerohash trust

> zerohash trust company llc (“zhtc”) is a non-depository trust company chartered by the North Carolina Commissioner of Banks. zhtc acts as a qualified custodian, with the authority to hold digital assets, stablecoin reserves, and tokenized securities in a fiduciary capacity.

Tokenization Engine

We provide a complete, end-to-end solution for issuing, managing, and distributing tokenized assets, including fungible (stablecoins) and non-fungible tokens (NFTs). Please refer to the Tokenization Engine integration guide [here](https://docs.zerohash.com/docs/tokenization-engine).

**Service Models**

- Issuer-as-a-Service: zerohash trust acts as your licensed issuer and Transfer Agent, handling all compliance, custody, and operational requirements. This is a turnkey solution for partners focused on distribution.
- Infrastructure-as-a-Service: Use your own regulated entity and reserves while leveraging our battle-tested tokenization infrastructure via API. This model is ideal for institutions requiring direct operational control.
