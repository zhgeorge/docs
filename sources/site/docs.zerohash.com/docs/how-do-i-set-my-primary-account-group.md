# Source: https://docs.zerohash.com/docs/how-do-i-set-my-primary-account-group

Your Primary Account Group can be thought of as your "go-to" [Account Group](https://docs.zerohash.com/docs/what-is-an-account-group). Perhaps this is the platform you trade on the most. The primary use case revolves around **deposits**. Funds will automatically be allocated to your Primary Account Group upon a deposit. This also applies to **withdrawals**\- withdrawals will be taken out of your Primary Account Group by default.

This can be configured by navigating to the **Administration** header, then the **Settings** sub-header, in the portal.
