# Source: https://docs.zerohash.com/docs/what-is-an-account-type

Account types refer to specific account utilizations in zerohash accounting ledgers. There are six account types maintained (`account_type`):

## 

Available

Asset balances that are free for use in trading, settling, paying fees or withdrawing. New deposits will flow into your Available account.

## 

Payable

Refers to a settlement amount you must pay that is outstanding due to having insufficient Available balance, or due to your counterparty having insufficient Available balance. This amount will be realized after all active trades have settled.

## 

Receivable

Refers to a settlement amount you will receive, but that has not been paid due to you having insufficient Available balance to fulfill your side, or due to your counterparty having insufficient Available balance to fulfill theirs. This amount will be paid after all active trades have settled.

## 

Collateral

Refers to assets that are currently being held for open positions, pending staking activation, or pending unstaking completion. These are actual funds, backed by deposits in one of zerohash's partner banks or custodians, that are locked until trades are closed or staking/unstaking requests are complete.

## 

Collateral Payable

Refers to the amount that is owed by the participant to cover collateral requirements.

## 

Staked

Refers to the amount that is actively staked and earning rewards. Staked assets must be unstaked before they can be sold or withdrawn.
