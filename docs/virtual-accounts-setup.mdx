## High Level - Integration Steps

1. Complete the Prerequisites below.
2. Onboard an end customer.
3. Link an external wallet address (required for the auto-convert Virtual Accounts).
4. Retrieve virtual accounts.
5. Manage virtual accounts.

## Pre-requisites

1. Work with zerohash to determine your **onboarding method**:
   1. **zerohash-managed KYC:** embed a zerohash-managed KYC SDK (docs [here](/docs/onboarding-experience-sample))
   2. **Reliance:** choose your own KYC/KYB provider, implement their tech, and submit the result to zerohash via API ([POST /participants/customers/new](https://docs.zerohash.com/reference/post_participants-customers-new) for individuals, [POST/participants/entity/new](https://docs.zerohash.com/reference/post_participants-entity-new) for businesses)

<Warning title="Approval needed">
The Reliance onboarding method requires approval by zerohash's Compliance team. Consult your zerohash representative to begin the review process.
</Warning>

2. In the [Client Portal](/docs/onboarding), create an **API key with the "Virtual Account" permission** enabled for Read and Write.
3. Provide zerohash with your **callback URL** so we can configure your Platform to listen to the following webhook events:
   1. [Participants:](https://docs.zerohash.com/reference/participants-1) for End customer status updates
   2. [External Account:](https://docs.zerohash.com/reference/external-account-status-updates) for external account status updates
      1. This is mostly relevant auto-convert Virtual Accounts, where the destination blockchain wallet address is considered an External Account.
   3. [Virtual Accounts:](https://docs.zerohash.com/reference/virtual-accounts) for dedicated virtual account status updates
4. Decide whether you or zerohash will send **email receipts** to your end customers. zerohash has a regulatory obligation to ensure end customers receive email receipts for certain virtual account events, and offers this as a free service to simplify your integration. See in-depth guide [here](https://docs.zerohash.com/docs/email-receipts-virtual-accounts).
