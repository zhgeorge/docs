Payins allow your platform to accept stablecoin and cryptocurrency payments from end users as a first-class payment method. Unlike card or ACH payments, on-chain transactions settle around the clock, are irreversible once confirmed, and work across borders without requiring local banking relationships. Zero Hash handles the on-chain infrastructure, compliance checks, and account crediting so your platform can focus on the customer experience.

## How the Auth Payins Flow Works

The Auth payins flow uses the Zero Hash deposit address infrastructure to receive funds from end users. A deposit address is generated per customer and per asset, and Zero Hash monitors it for incoming on-chain transactions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate a deposit address">
    Call `POST /deposits/digital_asset_addresses` with the customer's `participant_code` and the desired asset. Zero Hash returns a unique deposit address to relay to the end user.

    ```json
    // Request
    {
      "participant_code": "CUST01",
      "asset": "USDC"
    }

    // Response
    {
      "message": {
        "created_at": 1647050013,
        "address": "0x7f41A26C0CB9D5a1e57BE9cF10F8354b77CD8ab3",
        "participant_code": "CUST01",
        "asset": "USDC"
      }
    }
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customer sends crypto to the address">
    Share the deposit address with your end user. They initiate an on-chain send from their external wallet to the generated address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Zero Hash detects and confirms the transaction">
    Zero Hash monitors the address for incoming transactions. Once the deposit achieves the required on-chain confirmations, Zero Hash credits the funds to the customer's account automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor deposit activity">
    Poll `GET /deposits` with `include_customer_deposits=true` to track recent deposit activity. You can also subscribe to deposit webhooks for real-time notifications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customer balance is updated">
    After confirmation, the customer's balance is reflected in their Zero Hash account. Query `GET /accounts` with the customer's `participant_code` to verify the credited balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Supported Assets for Payins

Zero Hash supports an extensive range of stablecoins and crypto assets for payins, including:

- **USDC** — across 15 different blockchain networks
- **USDT** — across 4 different blockchain networks
- **PYUSD** — on Solana and Ethereum
- **RLUSD** — on Solana and XRPL
- **BTC, ETH, SOL** — and 30+ additional crypto assets
