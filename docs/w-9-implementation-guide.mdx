A W-9 is the IRS form that US persons — citizens, green-card holders, US tax residents, and US-formed entities — use to certify their US tax status and provide their Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN). A valid W-9 on file with Zero Hash exempts the participant from the 24% backup withholding applied to US-source income. This guide explains when W-9 collection is required, what data you need to collect in your product UI, and how to submit that data to Zero Hash via API.

<Note>
  Unlike the W-8 family of forms, the W-9 has no variants. The same form applies to every US person regardless of whether they are an individual or an entity, and regardless of entity type (corporation, partnership, LLC, trust, estate, etc.).
</Note>

## When Is W-9 Collection Required?

Collect W-9 data for every participant onboarded through Zero Hash's **US** flow who meets either of the following criteria:

- **Individuals** — Collect a W-9 when the participant is a US citizen, green-card holder, or US tax resident. A US citizen living abroad with a US tax ID still requires a W-9.
- **Entities** — Collect a W-9 when the entity is formed or organized under US law: US corporations, US partnerships, US LLCs, US trusts, US estates, and similar structures.

<Info>
  Participants onboarded through Zero Hash's EU or other non-US entities are governed by local tax documentation requirements and do not need a W-9 form.
</Info>

## If You Already Collect Certified W-9 Forms

If your platform already collects certified W-9 documents from customers, you do not need to rebuild your collection flow. Complete the following:

1. Upload the document via `POST /participants/documents` with the appropriate document type.
2. Ensure the participant record has `"is_w_form_certified": true` and a corresponding `w_form_certification` timestamp set at onboarding.
3. If the participant is subject to backup withholding, set `"is_not_subject_backup_withholding": false`.

Apply this retroactively for pre-existing customers and maintain it on an ongoing basis for new customers.

## Collect W-9 Fields in Your UI

If you do not already collect certified W-9 data, add the following fields to your customer onboarding journey. See the [Tax Data Collection via KYC-as-a-Service](/docs/tax-data-collection-kycaas) page if you want to understand how Zero Hash collects this data in its own KYC SDK.

### Required for all US individuals

- **TIN/SSN** — 9 digits, no dashes
- **Typed name as signature** — the customer's full legal name, used as their electronic signature
- **Mailing address** — only required if different from the residential address captured in your standard onboarding flow
- **Backup withholding indicator** — a checkbox asking whether the customer has been notified by the IRS that they are subject to backup withholding
- **W-9 attestation checkbox** — the customer confirms the standard IRS certification language (see [Section 2 of the IRS W-9 form](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/fw9.pdf))
- **Exemption codes (optional):**
  - `payee_exemption` — codes `1`–`13` or `NONE`
  - `fatca_reporting_exemption` — codes `A`–`M` or `NONE`

### Additional fields for US entities

- **Entity DBA name** — the trade name if different from the legal registered name
- **Entity type** — must be one of the following supported values:

| Value | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `C_CORPORATION` | C Corporation |
| `S_CORPORATION` | S Corporation |
| `PARTNERSHIP` | Partnership |
| `TRUST_ESTATE` | Trust or Estate |
| `LLC_C` | LLC taxed as a C Corporation |
| `LLC_P` | LLC taxed as a Partnership |
| `LLC_S` | LLC taxed as an S Corporation |
| `SOLE_PROPRIETOR` | Sole Proprietorship |
| `OTHER` | Other entity type |

## W-9 Field Reference

<ResponseField name="is_w_form_certified" type="boolean" required>
  Must be `true` when the participant has completed their W-9 certification. When `false`, the participant is subject to backup withholding. Always pair with a `w_form_certification` timestamp.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="w_form_certification" type="integer" required>
  Unix millisecond timestamp recording when the participant certified their W-9. Must accompany every `is_w_form_certified` submission.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="signature" type="string" required>
  The participant's full legal name as typed during electronic signature capture.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="signature_timestamp" type="integer" required>
  Unix millisecond timestamp of when the participant signed the W-9.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="is_not_subject_backup_withholding" type="boolean" required>
  Set to `true` as a positive attestation that the participant is **not** subject to IRS backup withholding. Set to `false` if they are subject to withholding.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="b_notice_receipt" type="boolean" required>
  Set to `false` unless the participant has been flagged in an IRS B-Notice as having mismatched name/TIN information. If `true`, the participant is subject to backup withholding until they re-certify.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="payee_exemption" type="string" required>
  Exempt payee code `1`–`13`, or `NONE`. Do not leave this field empty — use `NONE` for participants with no exemption.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="fatca_reporting_exemption" type="string" required>
  FATCA exemption code `A`–`M`, or `NONE`. Do not leave this field empty — use `NONE` for participants with no exemption.
</ResponseField>

## Handling Updates and Re-certification

When a participant's tax information changes — name change, address update, re-certification after a B-Notice — simply `PATCH` the participant endpoint with the updated values. Zero Hash handles re-validation on its side.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Always pair certification with a timestamp" icon="clock">
    Every time you send `is_w_form_certified`, include the `w_form_certification` timestamp. The same applies to `signature` and `signature_timestamp`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Use NONE, not empty, for exemptions" icon="ban">
    Always explicitly set `payee_exemption` and `fatca_reporting_exemption` to `NONE` when no exemption applies. Do not leave these fields blank.
  </Card>
  <Card title="B-Notices default to false" icon="envelope">
    Only set `b_notice_receipt` to `true` if you have received a B-Notice directly from the IRS or been notified by Zero Hash of an existing B-Notice against a customer account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Update when anything changes" icon="refresh">
    Name changes, address changes, or re-certifications all require a PATCH to the participant endpoint with the new values.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
