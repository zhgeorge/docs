The zerohash SDKs let you embed regulated stablecoins/crypto, payments, and onboarding flows directly into your product. Each SDK is a drop-in component that handles the regulated surface such as KYC, custody disclosures, signing, and compliance copy so your team can focus on the product wrapper around it.

ℹ️ **Note: All visuals and screenshots from the SDK documentation are conceptual only. Our SDKs are constantly updated and visuals may not reflect the current experience.**

## Available SDKs

- [Onboarding SDK](/docs/onboarding-experience-sample) - KYC and identity verification widget
- [Auth SDK](https://docs.zerohash.com/docs/auth-standalone) - link wallets with popular exchanges
- [Account Funding Deposits SDK](/docs/fund-integration-guide-onboarding-api-fund-sdk-sdk) - enable stablecoins/crypto funding to your platform account that are automatically converted to fiat upon receipt
- [Account Funding Withdrawals SDK](/docs/wallet-link-sdk-integration-guide) - enable fiat-funded withdrawals in stablecoins/crypto from your platform account to user exchanges or wallets
- [Crypto Deposits SDK](/docs/crypto-deposits-sdk-guide) - enable stablecoins/crypto funding to your platform account that are not automatically converted to fiat: assets remain in their original deposit type
- [Crypto Withdrawals](/docs/crypto-withdrawals-guide) - enable native stablecoins/crypto withdrawals to user exchanges or wallets
- [Payins SDK](/docs/payins-integration-guide) - accept payments via the Auth payins flow
- [Payouts SDK](/docs/payouts-sdk-integration-guide) - disburse funds via the Auth payouts flow
- [Mobile SDKs](/docs/mobile-sdks) - Native iOS / Android wrappers

## Choosing an SDK

Partners can start with the **Onboarding SDK** to satisfy KYC requirements, then layer on Buy/Sell, Payins, or Payouts depending on the use case. The SDKs share a common authentication scheme and theming layer (see Appearance & Branding) so adding a second SDK once the first is integrated is a small lift.

If your flow lives entirely server-side or you need finer control than the embedded widgets allow, use the REST API directly - see the [API Reference](https://docs.zerohash.com/reference-link/overview) and [Recipes](https://docs.zerohash.com/recipes).

## Prerequisites

- A zerohash Cert or Prod platform - see [Getting Started](/docs/getting-started)
