---
title: "KYC Support: Accepted Identification Documents"
description: "Supported documents if asked to provide a government issued photo ID"
---

You may be required to capture a government issued photo ID. At a baseline, IDs must have visible, extractable data available, clear portraits (photo of you), and they must not be expired.

The most commonly accepted ID types globally are:

- Driver's License
- Passport
- State ID (similar to driver's license but without driving privileges)
