After you submit your platform application to Zero Hash, it passes through a structured multi-step review before you receive final approval or rejection. Understanding the process helps you respond quickly to any requests from the Zero Hash team and avoid unnecessary delays. The review is conducted by the Zero Hash AML/KYC staff and culminates in a formal decision by the Zero Hash Participant Committee.

## Review Stages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Application Submitted">
    You submit your completed platform application, including all required supporting documentation. Once the application is received, Zero Hash notifies every **authorized signer** listed on the application.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorized Signer Review and Attestation">
    Each authorized signer must individually review the application for accuracy and attest that all information is current and correct. Zero Hash will not accept the application as complete until **all** authorized signers have reviewed and attested. This step is entirely on your side — check with your signers early to avoid a bottleneck.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AML/KYC Staff Review">
    Once the application is fully submitted and signed, the Zero Hash AML/KYC compliance staff begins their review of your information and all attached supporting documentation. During this stage, Zero Hash staff may contact you to request missing or incomplete information. Respond promptly — delays in providing additional documentation extend the overall timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Participant Committee Review">
    After the AML/KYC review is complete, the application is forwarded to the Zero Hash **Participant Committee** for a final review. The Committee makes the formal decision to approve or reject the application.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decision Communicated">
    Zero Hash will communicate the Committee's decision — approval or rejection — to your team. If approved, you will receive access to the Cert portal and can begin your technical integration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Documentation You Need

The specific documents required vary based on your platform's entity type and jurisdiction, but all applications typically require:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Business Formation Documents" icon="building">
    Articles of incorporation, certificate of formation, or equivalent document establishing your legal entity.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ownership and Control Information" icon="sitemap">
    Details on all beneficial owners (typically those with 25%+ ownership) and control persons, including names, dates of birth, and government-issued ID.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AML/KYC Program Documentation" icon="shield">
    A written description of your Anti-Money Laundering and Know Your Customer program, including how you identify and onboard your end users.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Business Description" icon="file-lines">
    A clear description of your business model, the products you plan to build on Zero Hash, and the end-user populations you serve.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Tips for a Smooth Review

<Tip>
  Ensure all authorized signers are aware they will receive a notification and need to act before the review can proceed. Identifying all signers and confirming their contact information before you submit the application saves significant time.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  When Zero Hash staff requests additional documentation, respond as quickly as possible. Each round of back-and-forth adds time to the overall review cycle.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Zero Hash will reach out via email if additional information is needed during the AML/KYC review stage. Monitor the inbox associated with the contacts listed on your application, and ensure emails from `@zerohash.com` are not filtered as spam.
</Note>

## After Approval

Once the Participant Committee approves your application, Zero Hash will:

1. Provision your account in the **Cert (certification) portal** at [portal.cert.zerohash.com](https://portal.cert.zerohash.com).
2. Provide your platform code (a six-character alphanumeric identifier, e.g., `PLAT01`) that you will use in all API calls.
3. Give you access to the Zero Hash API documentation and Cert environment so you can begin building and testing your integration.

Your Cert environment is pre-funded with test float so you can exercise the full transaction flow without moving real money.
