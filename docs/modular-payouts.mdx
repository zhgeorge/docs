## Overview

Modular Payouts is a 4-step workflow that lets your platform own and manage payout state from payor onboarding through final settlement. With this solution you get full transparency and control at each stage, making it ideal for platforms with complex workflows, compliance requirements, or custom UX needs.

### The 4-Step Workflow:

1. Onboard Payor - Register the payer (if applicable)
2. Onboard Beneficiary - Register the payout recipient
3. Link Beneficiary External Account - Connect beneficiary to blockchain wallet or external account
4. Initiate Payout - Execute the transaction

### Which Modular Payouts solution works best for you?

Build custom payout experiences, track state at every step, or operate with zero technical overhead. You choose.
