The zerohash Agentic Finance Suite is a full-stack solution for businesses looking to provision agents with the tools to manage onchain money.

## Hold Onchain Money

zerohash provides the Know Your Agent (KYA) screening that ties the agent's operator to the money they interact with. zerohash also allows businesses and individuals to delegate and manage permissions for agentic flows, unlocking access to more than 40 protocols and chains and 100+ assets.

## Move Onchain Money

zerohash provides a simple, global and instant mechanism for agents and businesses to move money. x402 support unlocks the ability for money to be programmable like software, with the money movement becoming part of the HTTP request/response flow.

## Stream Onchain Money

Streaming introduces session authorization with a spend cap, and the Payments Streaming Engine meters actual usage and settles in increments as the session runs, crediting the business in near real time and never charging beyond what was consumed.

## Use Cases

- **Agent pays for content or data access for a human:** A research agent unlocks a paywalled article, dataset, or report at the moment of access, paying per-item instead of requiring a pre-existing subscription.
- **Agent pays another API for data it needs to complete its task:** A research or trading agent calls a live weather, market-data, or geolocation API and pays per-request, rather than the API owner needing to manage it as a subscriber.
- **Agent pays another AI model for inference it can't run itself:** A customer-service or orchestration agent calls out to a specialized model - translation, image classification, a larger reasoning model — paying per-call or per-token for that specific piece of work.
- **Agent pays for infrastructure it's actively consuming:** A workload or device-management agent pays for compute, bandwidth, or storage as it scales resources up or down, settling the charge as part of provisioning rather than through a monthly invoice.
