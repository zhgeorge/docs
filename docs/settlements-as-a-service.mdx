Zero Hash Settlements as a Service lets you offload the entire post-trade lifecycle — calculation, movement, and on-chain delivery — to a regulated infrastructure provider. Whether you source liquidity externally and drop trades into Zero Hash for settlement, negotiate large OTC blocks with institutional counterparties, or swap between digital asset pairs, Zero Hash acts as both Calculation Agent and Settlement Agent under its FinCEN-registered Money Service Business (MSB) license and money transmitter authorizations in all 50 U.S. states.

## Settlement Flow

The end-to-end settlement process moves through six stages from trade execution to on-chain confirmation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trade Execution">
    Trades are executed on your platform or a supported venue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trade Delivery to Zero Hash">
    Trades are delivered to Zero Hash via API, phone, instant message, or any other agreed channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Obligation Calculation">
    Upon receipt, Zero Hash calculates margin and settlement obligations as the neutral Calculation Agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Internal Movement">
    Zero Hash, as a FinCEN-registered MSB and money transmitter, executes fiat and digital asset movements internally across participant ledger accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="On-Chain Delivery">
    Zero Hash isolates digital asset movements, compresses them for efficiency, and broadcasts the final settlement to the blockchain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirmation">
    All stakeholders can verify their ownership of digital assets on-chain, providing a transparent, immutable settlement record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Trading Models

Zero Hash supports two distinct operating models. Choose the one that matches how your platform interacts with trade counterparties.

### Agency / Broker Exchange Model

In the Agency or Broker Exchange Model, you facilitate trades between counterparties without taking any principal risk. You do not trade on your own balance sheet — you act as an intermediary that connects buyers and sellers.

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| **Calculation Agent** | Neutral third party (Zero Hash) that calculates obligations for each leg of the trade |
| **Settlement Agent** | Zero Hash, which executes the movement of fiat currency and digital assets |
| **Platform** | An exchange, RFQ system, brokerage network, dealer platform, or combination thereof |
| **Platform Operator** | You — the facilitator connecting counterparties without taking principal exposure |

**Flow diagram:** Your platform matches buyer and seller → trade details submitted to Zero Hash → Zero Hash calculates net obligations for each counterparty → assets and fiat move simultaneously under DVP → all parties receive on-chain confirmation.

<Note>
  In the Agency Model, Zero Hash's calculation and settlement role provides a neutral, regulated layer between your counterparties, reducing operational complexity and compliance burden for your organization.
</Note>

### Principal Dealer Model

In the Principal Dealer Model, you act as the counterparty to all trades — buying and selling digital assets on your own balance sheet. You take principal risk on each transaction and manage inventory positions.

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| **Calculation Agent** | Neutral third party (Zero Hash) that calculates your net obligations across all trades |
| **Settlement Agent** | Zero Hash, which executes movement of fiat and digital assets |
| **Platform** | Your trading system — you originate all buy and sell sides |
| **Platform Operator** | You — acting as principal dealer with full inventory and price risk |

**Flow diagram:** You quote and execute trades as principal → net position is submitted to Zero Hash → Zero Hash calculates your aggregate delivery obligation → net settlement executed in a single movement → on-chain delivery confirms finality.

<Tip>
  The Principal Dealer Model is well suited to platforms that want full control over pricing and spreads while delegating all post-trade clearing and settlement operations to Zero Hash.
</Tip>

## API Reference

Use the following endpoints to submit trades and manage digital asset addresses:

| Endpoint | Description |
|---|---|
| `POST /trades` | Submit a trade for Zero Hash settlement |
| `GET /trades` | Query all trades for your platform |
| `GET /trades/:id` | Retrieve a specific trade by ID |
| `POST /deposits/digital_asset_addresses` | Create a deposit address for on-chain receipt |
| `GET /deposits/digital_asset_addresses` | List existing deposit addresses |
| `POST /withdrawals/requests` | Submit a withdrawal request |
| `GET /withdrawals/requests` | List all withdrawal requests |
| `GET /withdrawals/requests/:id` | Retrieve a specific withdrawal request |

<Warning>
  All trades submitted to Zero Hash must include both counterparty `participant_code` values and the asset/amount for each side. Incomplete trade submissions will be rejected and must be resubmitted.
</Warning>
