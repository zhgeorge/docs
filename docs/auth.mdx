## Overview & Concepts

**AUTH enables direct account-to-account transfers between custodial accounts (i.e., exchanges and fintechs) and non-custodial accounts.**

Backed by a deep network of supported venues, AUTH eliminates manual and error-prone transfer steps that lead to fragmented user experiences. Our platform handles secure authentication, initiates transfers, and ensures visibility and adherence to key compliance and AML standards. Offered through easy-to-integrate SDKs, AUTH is built for scale, security, and seamless user journeys.

## Supported Custody Models & Assets

AUTH supports both custodial solutions, such as brokerages like Gemini, as well as non-custodial solutions such as Metamask wallets.

You can review our full list of supported AUTH Providers [here](/docs/auth-network).

## Compliance & Name Matching

AUTH supports two versions of our transaction flow depending on platform requirements and integration pattern: a tech-only solution and a more robust name-matching service.

AUTH **Lite** - provides tech connectivity without account-matching validation between the end-user account and the external account being connected

- Enables faster and more flexible integrations with minimal friction for end users

- Works best for low-risk or closed-loop environments where account ownership verification is not required

AUTH **Validate -** Performs account matching between the KYC’d customer record and the external account being connected

- Adds a layer of identity verification to reduce the risk of misdirected transfers or fraud
- Supports compliance with internal policies or regulatory requirements that require account ownership validation

## Supported Configurations

Tailor your AUTH setup using the available configuration options:

- **Custodial vs. non-custodial accounts** - configure whether zerohash AUTH SDK displays Custodial accounts(e.g., Gemini or Robinhood exchanges), Non-Custodial Accounts (e.g. Metamask or Phantom wallet), or both
- **AUTH Provider configurations** - fine-grain control for enabling specific custodial and non-custodial accounts by product
- **Asset & blockchain network control** - specify which assets and networks you wish to support for your platform and your customers, by product-line (e.g. Fund, Withdrawals, etc.)
- **Block non-AUTH initiated transfers** - specify whether you want to only accept deposits that have been initiated through our product, ensuring that only transfers from the correct, verified (KYC'd) customer are credited to their account
- **Customizable name matching logic** - Tailor the strength of our name matching algorithm to find the right balance of speed and accuracy.
